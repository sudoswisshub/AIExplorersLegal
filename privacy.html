Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to AI Explorers! We build educational and engaging programming games for children. We believe privacy is extremely important, especially when it comes to kids. Our policy is simple: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

1. Personal Information

We do not collect any personal information (such as name, address, email address, or phone number) from children using the app. The only email address we ever handle is if a parent contacts our support team directly via email.

2. Locally Stored Data

All gameplay progress (stars earned, levels completed, purchased character customization items, unlocked concept cards) is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store this information on external servers.

3. COPPA Compliance

Our app is designed to be fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not include any tracking tools, third-party analytics, advertisements, or in-app purchases that collect user details.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not share any data with third parties since we do not collect any data in the first place.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at alice.turcanu91@gmail.com.
